Primons

Primons are Reality Cascade's foundational currency: a fictional "pre-particle" representing raw quantum potential, existing for a fraction of a Planck length before it decides what it wants to become. Nothing in real physics is quite a Primon, but the idea borrows from genuine concepts like Planck-scale quantum foam and vacuum fluctuations and regions of spacetime where virtual particles flicker in and out of existence before settling back into nothing.

Production: Primons += (Primons/s × 1000 ⁄ Primon Speed) every tick, scaled by your Primon Multiplier and Achievement Bonus multipliers.
